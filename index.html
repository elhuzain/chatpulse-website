Commands

Five commands. That's it.

Slash commands
/set-language Choose the language ChatPulse writes its messages in.
/set-channel Pick the channel ChatPulse watches and posts in.
/revive-chat Post a conversation starter right now, without waiting.
/invite Get the link to add ChatPulse to another server.
/help See every command and how to use it.
